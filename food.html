Estimates only. Values come from USDA FoodData Central, scaled to portions you gave or to stated assumptions, and cover only the meals logged (breakfast is not normally eaten, so none is expected; unlogged snacks are not counted). Calcium shown is the amount eaten, not the amount absorbed (for example, spinach calcium is poorly absorbed). Each day shows food, supplements and combined separately. Supplement amounts are label values times the schedule on the Supplements page. Every-other-day items (liver, glucosamine, collagen) are averaged at half a dose per day because their start day is not logged; iodine and B12 count only Mon/Wed/Fri, vitamin D3+K2 only Mon-Fri. Collagen calories are not on the label, so they are excluded. Supplement magnesium upper limit of 350 mg/day (NIH ODS) applies to supplement magnesium only, not food. Ranges reflect portion uncertainty.
