Mock: week-at-a-glance charts

Ten ways to show the whole week on one view.

mock Experimental chart gallery, not part of the main site. ← Back to Food Log

How to read: every nutrient is shown as % of its daily target (protein 56 g, fiber 30 g, calcium 1,000 mg, potassium 3,400 mg, magnesium 420 mg). *Sodium is vs its 2,300 mg upper limit, so lower is better. Calories have no target and are omitted (except in chart 10). Uses the real logged data for Oct 10 to Oct 16 (7 complete days); Oct 17 is still in progress, drawn dashed, hollow or hatched, and left out of averages and the radar. Food values exclude items with no data, so some totals are minimums. Estimates only.
Show:

New) Overlay bar new

One bar per day; every nutrient is its own colored layer starting at the left, longest at the back so none is hidden. Hover, tap or Tab onto a layer for the numbers.

Scale:

Scale: % of each nutrient’s daily target (protein 56 g, fiber 30 g, calcium 1,000 mg, potassium 3,400 mg, magnesium 420 mg). Sodium* is against its 2,300 mg limit, so shorter is better. Dashed magenta line = 100%. Bars past the right edge are clipped.

Scale: raw numbers on one shared axis, as logged (mg for calcium, potassium, magnesium and sodium; g for protein and fiber). Fiber and protein, in grams, look tiny next to potassium in mg, which is why % mode is the default. Tiny layers are given a minimum width so they stay visible.
