Ten ways to show the whole week on one view.

mock Experimental chart gallery, not part of the main site. ← Back to Food Log

How to read: every nutrient is shown as % of its daily target (protein 56 g, fiber 30 g, calcium 1,000 mg, potassium 3,400 mg, magnesium 420 mg). *Sodium is vs its 2,300 mg upper limit, so lower is better. Calories have no target and are omitted (except in chart 10). Uses the real logged data for Oct 10 to Oct 17 (8 complete days); Oct 17 is still in progress, drawn dashed, hollow or hatched, and left out of averages and the radar. Food values exclude items with no data, so some totals are minimums. Estimates only.

New) Overlay bar new

One bar per day; every nutrient is its own colored layer starting at the left, longest at the back so none is hidden. Hover, tap or Tab onto a layer for the numbers.

Amount:

Absorbed view: only calcium is adjusted, to estimated absorbed (typical adult; milk 27%, spinach 5%, cabbage/broccoli 27%, others an assumed 25%; NIH ODS, National Academies 2011, accessed 2026-10-17); the faint dashed outline = calcium eaten. All other nutrients: absorption not adjusted. Target lines are unchanged because targets are for total eaten.

Scale: % of each nutrient’s daily target (protein 56 g, fiber 30 g, calcium 1,000 mg, potassium 3,400 mg, magnesium 420 mg). Sodium* is against its 2,300 mg limit, so shorter is better. Dashed magenta line = 100%. Bars past the right edge are clipped.

Scale: raw numbers on one shared axis, as logged (mg for calcium, potassium, magnesium and sodium; g for protein and fiber). Fiber and protein, in grams, look tiny next to potassium in mg, which is why % mode is the default. Tiny layers are given a minimum width so they stay visible.

Eaten vs absorbed (calcium mockups) new

Four ways to show calcium eaten next to calcium absorbed, using the real logged data, food only (supplements have no calcium). All absorbed numbers are ESTIMATES for a typical adult, not personal values; these mockups ignore the food/supplement toggle.

Assumptions (estimates): absorbed = eaten × an assumed percentage per food. Sourced (NIH ODS calcium fact sheet, link, accessed 2026-10-17): milk ~27%, spinach ~5%, and low-oxalate greens such as broccoli, kale and cabbage described as similar to milk (27% used). Assumed default ~25% (adult average per National Academies 2011 report) for everything else: pumpkin seeds, lentils, walnuts, fish, eggs, bread, yogurt, kefir, skyr and so on (NIH gives ~30% for dairy and fortified foods, but I kept the default as marked). Hot chocolate is treated as milk (27%, assumed). Real absorption varies with dose, age and the rest of the meal. There is no official “absorbed requirement”; the dashed ~250 mg line (25% × 1,000 mg) is illustrative arithmetic, not an official figure.
